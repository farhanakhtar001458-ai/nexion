# Disclaimer / Licence

## Kya hai ye archive

RGPV (Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya, Bhopal) ke **B.Tech first year** ke question papers ka
student-run archive hai — mid-sem aur end-sem preparation ke liye.

## Copyright aur ownership

- **Question papers RGPV ke intellectual property hain.** In papers ka content humne banaya nahi hai.
- `papers/original-archive/` ki PDFs public paper-archive mirror repositories se collect ki gayi hain
  (primary index: <https://www.rgpvonline.com/rgpv-first-year.html>, mirror credit:
  `littlechampsacademy/rgpv-question-paper.littlechampsacademy.in`). Ye unofficial mirrors hain, RGPV se affiliated nahi.
- `papers/reconstructed-2022-2026/` ki PDFs me question text public paper pages se liya gaya hai; PDF layout/formatting
  is archive ke maintainers ne banaya hai. Hindi version aur exact layout ke liye original paper hi authentic source hai.
- Repo ka **original code/content** (guide HTML, scripts, analysis) free use ke liye hai — credit de dena accha lagega.

## Intended use

Sirf **personal study, teaching aur exam preparation** ke liye. Papers ko bechna, paywall ke peeche lagana, ya
commercial use karna allowed nahi hai.

## Takedown / removal requests

Agar aap RGPV ke authorised representative hain, kisi paper ke rights-holder hain, ya kisi content par aapko
objection hai — is repo me **issue khol dein** (ya maintainer ko contact karein). Valid request par content
**turant remove** kar diya jayega, koi sawaal nahi.

## Accuracy

- Papers jaise-mile-waise post kiye gaye hain; hum accuracy claim nahi karte.
- Mid-sem papers **college-level internal** hote hain aur public nahi hote — is archive me jo mid-sem analysis hai
  wo end-sem papers ke repeat-pattern par based hai (best available predictor).
- Exam se pehle apne college ka **official syllabus/circular** verify kar lena.

_Ye repository kisi bhi tarah se RGPV dwara endorsed ya approved nahi hai._
