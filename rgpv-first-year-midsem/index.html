Pehle ye samajh lo — Mid-Sem kaise hota hai

Total marks
End-Sem 70 + Mid-Sem test 20 + Quiz/Assignment 10 = 100 (RGPV scheme, 70-20-10)
Mid-sem kitne
Har subject me kam se kam 2 mid-sem test conduct hote hain (MSE-1, MSE-2). College ke hisaab se best/average count hota hai.
MSE-1 syllabus
Ghar-ghar thoda alag, par lagbhag sab college Unit 1 + Unit 2 rakhhte hain. MSE-2 me Unit 3-5.
MSE ka paper
Internal (college) paper — RGPV publish nahi karta. Isliye yahan RGPV end-sem papers (2020-2026) se unit-wise repeat analysis banaya gaya hai, jo MSE ke questions ka sabse sahi predictor hai.
Answer length
MSE me generally 2-3 long (6-8 marks) + short notes aate hain. Derivation + diagram + formula + 1 numerical = full marks.
AIML note
AI/ML ke core papers (AL-304 Artificial Intelligence, AL-405 Machine Learning) 3rd-4th sem se shuru hote hain. 1st year me sirf common subjects hain.
SET ka confusion clear kar lo: RGPV first year me do set chhalte hain — SET-A (CS, CSIT, EE, EX) aur SET-B (ME, CE, EC, AIML, CY, IP, AU). AIML SET-B me hai, matlab aapke Sem-1 me Physics group (BT-201/202/203/204/205) hai. Agar aapke college ne AIML ko SET-A me rakha hai to aapke Sem-1 me BT-101 Chemistry, BT-102 Maths-I, BT-103 English, BT-104 BEEE, BT-105 Graphics honge — uske saare paper links bhi guide me diye hain ("Sem-2 / SET-A" tab).

Must-memorize "sure-shot" list (sabse zyada repeat)

Derivations / Numericals

  • Uncertainty principle (gamma-ray microscope) · TISE + particle in 1-D box (energy eigenvalues) · Newton's rings numerical · Grating resolving power · NA & acceptance angle of fiber · Variation of parameters (y''+y = tan x / cosec x) · Cauchy–Euler equation · Levelling by HI & Rise-Fall method · Fe-Fe3C diagram phases · Bernoulli equation derivation.

Theory/Short notes jo roz aate hain

  • Physical significance of wave function · Rayleigh criterion · Michelson interferometer working · Optical pyrometer · Vernier calliper / sine bar / slip gauge · Functions of OS · System vs application software · OOP characteristics & POP vs OOP · Types of inheritance · Cement tests & bricks properties · Stone masonry types · Theodolite parts · Remote sensing applications.

Common galtiyan (jisme marks katte hain)

  • Derivation ka diagram + assumption chhod dena — RGPV me diagram ke bina aadhe marks jaate hain.
  • Maths me arbitrary constants (c1, c2) aur "Hence proved" line na likhna.
  • Physics numerical me units (nm, Å, eV) convert na karna.
  • BCEM levelling numerical me arithmetic check (ΣBS − ΣFS = ΣRise − ΣFall = Last RL − First RL) na likhna — ye ek mark pakka.
  • BCE me C++ program me #include<iostream.h> / syntax galat likhna.
